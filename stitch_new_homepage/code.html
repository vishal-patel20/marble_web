Expert Insights

Frequently asked questions about material selection and installation.

Marble is a 100% natural stone renowned for its unique, non-repeating veining and classical beauty. It is porous and requires periodic sealing. Quartz is an engineered stone made from 90% crushed quartz mixed with resin, offering a non-porous, highly durable, and maintenance-free surface with consistent patterning.

Yes, we operate a state-of-the-art fabrication facility. We offer fully bespoke services including custom edge profiles, specialized cutting for integrated sinks, precise book-matching for large wall installations, and detailed architectural stonework.

Natural stone should be cleaned with mild, pH-neutral soap and water. Avoid acidic cleaners (like vinegar or lemon) which can etch the surface. We recommend professionally resealing marble and granite every 1-2 years depending on usage to protect against staining.
